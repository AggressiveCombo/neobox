# Credits

Third-party assets shipped with neobox, with their authors, licences and source pages. The About screen (`client/src/ui/screens/about.ts`, `THIRD_PARTY`) lists the same entries. Add a section per asset group and keep both places in step.

## Music

### Menu music: `client/public/music/menu.mp3`

- Title: "Menu Loop"
- Author: Umplix
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/menu-loop-0 (WAV file `menu_loop_0.wav`)
- Changes: transcoded to 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 128 kb/s, loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS integrated, 3 s fade-out at the end.

## Data

### Emoji shortcodes: `crates/server/src/emoji.tsv`

- Dataset: gemoji (`db/emoji.json`), Copyright (c) GitHub, Inc.
- Licence: MIT, https://github.com/github/gemoji/blob/master/LICENSE
- Source: https://github.com/github/gemoji (generated by `tools/emoji/gen.mjs`)

### Profanity word list: `client/src/profanity-words.ts`

- Dataset: List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words (English), LDNOOBW
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source: https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words (generated by `tools/profanity/gen.mjs`)

### IP geolocation for room distances and flags (server)

- Dataset: DB-IP "IP to City Lite" (MaxMind format). Not bundled: the operator points `NEOBOX_GEOIP` at the database.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source: https://db-ip.com/db_lite.php
